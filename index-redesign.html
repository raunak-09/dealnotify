FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is the price comparison?
We use AI matching to make sure we compare the same product, not a knockoff or wrong size. If we can't confidently identify a match, we say so — we'd rather show you nothing than show you a wrong comparison.
What retailers does Compare work on?
Compare works as a target on Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, and eBay. As a source (the page you're on when you trigger Compare), Amazon and Walmart are fully supported, with eBay coming soon.
Does Compare slow down my browsing?
No. Comparisons run in the background; the panel only appears when results are ready (typically 2–5 seconds). If a retailer is slow, we skip it and show you the others — no spinner of doom.
Is my browsing data tracked?
Only the product URL is sent to our servers, only when you're on a supported retailer's product page. We don't see your other tabs, your search history, or anything outside the supported retailers. Read our privacy policy for the details.
Do you make money from the affiliate links?
Yes — when you click "View at Walmart" and buy, we may earn a small commission. This never affects which retailer we show you. We always rank by lowest price, period.
Can I sign up with Google?
Yes. The Chrome extension supports Sign in with Google. Your email is auto-verified by Google, so you can start using Compare immediately — no email-verification round-trip required.
