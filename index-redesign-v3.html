Common questions

Do I need the Chrome extension?
No — the web app works on its own. But the extension makes everything faster: comparisons appear automatically as you shop, and you can set price-drop or restock alerts in one click without leaving the product page.
How does the price comparison work?
When you're on a supported product page, DealNotify identifies the product and searches the same item across other supported stores. If we find a confident match somewhere cheaper, we show it with the savings amount and a direct link.
How fast are price-drop alerts?
On the Free plan, every 6 hours. On Pro, every 2 hours. The moment your target price hits, an email is sent within seconds.
What retailers does DealNotify support?
Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, and eBay. Compare works across all six; price-drop and restock alerts work on any product URL from these retailers.
Is my browsing data private?
Yes. The extension only sends product URLs from the six supported retailer pages. We don't see other tabs, search history, or anything outside those retailers. Read our privacy policy for details.
Can I cancel the Pro plan?
Yes, anytime from your dashboard. Pro is month-to-month with no contract. If you cancel, your account stays on the Free plan and your tracked products keep working — just at the slower 6-hour check interval.
