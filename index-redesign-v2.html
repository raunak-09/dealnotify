Common questions

Do I need the Chrome extension?
No. The web app works on its own — paste any product URL, set a target price, and you'll get email alerts. The extension just makes it easier to track from any product page and shows price comparisons automatically while you shop.
How does the price comparison work?
When you're on a supported product page (or paste a URL), DealNotify identifies the product (brand, model, key specs) and searches the same item across the other supported stores. If we find a confident match somewhere cheaper, we show it.
How fast are price-drop alerts?
On the free plan, we check every 6 hours. On Pro, every 2 hours. The moment the price hits your target, an email goes out within seconds. For most shoppers, that's well within the window of a typical sale.
What if the item isn't sold at all 6 stores?
No problem. We compare against whichever stores carry the item. If a product is exclusive to one retailer, the comparison will simply show no other matches — and you can still set a price-drop or restock alert.
Is my browsing data private?
Yes. The extension only sends us the product URL of the page you're on, and only when that page is one of the 6 supported retailers. We don't see other tabs, search history, or anything outside those retailers. Read our privacy policy for details.
Can I cancel?
Yes, anytime, from your dashboard. Pro is month-to-month with no contract. If you cancel, your account stays on the free plan and your tracked products keep working — just at the slower 6-hour check interval.
